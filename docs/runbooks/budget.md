# Spending and provider admission

The personal product has no commercial tiers or workspace allowance API. Hosted private processing uses current direct-owner/project authorization, verified immutable profile IDs, explicit processing consent and a shared US$20 monthly application cap. Admission months use Asia/Calcutta. Keys alone do not prove billing/account/capability/pricing or authorize private processing.

Reserve conservatively and durably journal operation/body identity before HTTP. Bound token/response/attempt sizes; disable transport retries, redirects and environment proxies. Record actual usage when known; unknown completion retains at least the conservative claim and is never replayed automatically. Rejected answers and lost acknowledgements do not erase spending. Overruns are recorded and disable unsafe underbounded profiles. Reconciliation is explicit/idempotent; do not edit ledger history to retry.

Retained financial tables/functions and compatibility/public fixed-input tools still reference workspace-backed records internally. Their tests verify active accounting/preservation boundaries, not obsolete product HTTP. Fixed synthetic/public authority never authorizes arbitrary private payloads; the distinct lifetime maxima remain US$0.25 synthetic and US$1 public, subject to global admission. No paid evaluation rerun is implied by source changes.

Current checks live in `backend/tests/providers/test_budget.py`, compatibility/probe/public-budget suites and `backend/tests/personal/test_personal_admission.py`. Real account verification and preserved-data cutover remain in [tasks](../tasks.md). Historical spending measurements are only in [memory](../memory.md).
